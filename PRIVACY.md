# Privacy Policy — NoteEase

**Last updated:** May 11, 2026

## Overview

NoteEase is a lightweight Chrome extension for taking quick notes directly from the browser toolbar. This privacy policy explains how NoteEase handles your data.

---

## Data Collection

**NoteEase does not collect any personal data.**

- No user accounts required
- No data is sent to external servers
- No analytics or tracking of any kind
- No cookies

---

## Data Storage

All notes and preferences (such as theme settings) are stored **locally on your device** using the Chrome browser's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API.

- Your notes never leave your browser
- Data is only accessible by you on your device
- Uninstalling the extension will delete all stored data

---

## Permissions

NoteEase requests only one permission:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Save your notes and theme preference locally in the browser |

No other permissions are requested or required.

---

## Third Parties

NoteEase does **not** share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties. There are no third-party services, SDKs, or APIs integrated into this extension.

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new date.
